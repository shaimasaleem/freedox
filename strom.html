About Our Events

St Aloysius University has been actively involved in various events related to its B.Tech programs. The university has launched AICTE-approved B.Tech programs and has organized induction programs for first-year students to familiarize them with the expectations of engineering education and equip them with essential leadership and industry-oriented skills. The university also holds national-level undergraduate fests like 'Aloysian Fest 2025' to celebrate its rich legacy and engage with the community. These events reflect the university's commitment to academic excellence, institutional innovation, and holistic student development.
